\begin{abstract}
These notes record a just-for-fun attempt to see how far AI can push an
integer-multiplication bound. They document a hobby project, not a research
paper. The generated argument is largely unverified: the accompanying scripts
check finite identities and arithmetic, but do not validate the full mathematical
argument. The work builds on Douglas Colkitt's repository and OpenAI's paper,
cited below.

The proposed change replaces the side correction circuit in the complex finite network by
reusable sums of triple indicators sharing two fixed points.  Within each
batch these indicators are orthonormal over $\mathbb F_2$.  A bounded batch
size provides explicit norm-one witnesses for every new complementary
residual, in both circuit orientations.  Together with a redundant-center
elimination and first/third-stage auxiliary reuse, the construction at
$h=24$ has $4{,}496{,}369{,}044{,}992$ roles and supports the complex
saving $\ac=4\cdot10^{-9}$.  The previously constructed bit network is
unchanged.  Under the retained compact-control and multiplication
interfaces, exact rational parameters give
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\right),\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{5929220328}{10^{19}}
       =5.929220328\cdot10^{-10}>2^{-31}.
\]
This is about $7.14364$ times the saving at the repository revision
examined.  These are conditional asymptotic exponent comparisons,
not measured runtime improvements.  The proof and exact certificates do
not independently establish the complete upstream theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Baseline, dependencies, and the change}
The baseline is Douglas Colkitt's \emph{integer-mult-bounds}~\cite{baseline}, commit
\begin{center}\texttt{6e564879f51ae16f23d392e9e196c605f36d90df}.\end{center}
Its strongest stated witness is $83/10^{12}=8.3\cdot10^{-11}>2^{-34}$.
This is newer than the earlier $2^{-59}$ witness.  The baseline retains
OpenAI's \emph{Integer multiplication below $n\log n$}~\cite{upstream}, pinned at
\begin{center}\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}.\end{center}
The model is a fixed finite alphabet on a fixed finite number of
one-dimensional Turing tapes.  Source attribution, licenses, and the
pinned manuscript are preserved.

The present result assumes the pinned manuscript's stream operations,
bit-transfer and complex-phase transfer, analytic estimates, prime
selection, Gaussian resampling, synthetic transforms, and final rounding.
It also retains the repository's paired $h=50$ bit circuit, nonadjacent
routing, compact dirty-control movement, complete temporary reservations,
local exceptional repair, generalized stopping guard, and tightened Gaussian
choice.  The latter written arguments have the same conditional status
here as in the baseline.  The new mathematical work concerns the finite
complex circuit, its two binary frame assignments, and the parameter
comparisons resulting from that circuit.

The old complex motif at $h=25$ certifies only
$\ac=4.18\cdot10^{-10}$, making its stopped leaves the limiting term.
The new motif certifies $\ac=4\cdot10^{-9}$, larger than the retained
bit saving.  This changes which term controls the completed layer.

\section{Exact recurrence exponents}
The complex counts above give
\[
 \eta_{\rm c}=1-\frac{s}{Wm}=\frac{37}{948319488}.
\]
The supplied exact logarithm enclosure proves
\[
 \log m<\frac{9535}{1000},\qquad
 \eta_{\rm c}>\frac4{10^9}\frac{9535}{1000}.
\]
For $x>0$, $e^{-x}>1-x$.  Hence
\[
 m^{1-\ac}
 =m e^{-\ac\log m}
 >m(1-\ac\log m)>m(1-\eta_{\rm c})=\frac{s}{W},
 \qquad\ac=\frac4{10^9}.
\]
This establishes the required strict complex recurrence exponent
$\sigma=1-\ac$.  An exact enclosure of the actual recurrence saving is
approximately $4.0922725765\cdot10^{-9}$, but the simpler certified
$4\cdot10^{-9}$ already suffices.

The bit graph remains the existing paired construction at $h_{\rm b}=50$,
with $m_{\rm b}=125000$ and exact relative deficit
\[
 \eta_{\rm b}=\frac{23}{661055000}.
\]
For the readable witness below one may retain $\ab=296/10^{11}$.
For the slightly stronger final witness, certify
\[
 \ab=\frac{29646101715649}{10^{22}}
      =2.9646101715649\cdot10^{-9}.
\]
If $L_{\rm b}^{+}$ is the supplied rational upper bound on
$\log(125000)$, exact arithmetic establishes
\[
 \eta_{\rm b}+\frac{\eta_{\rm b}^2}{2}
      >\ab L_{\rm b}^{+}.
\]
The positive Taylor tail gives
$-\log(1-\eta_{\rm b})>\eta_{\rm b}+\eta_{\rm b}^2/2$.
It follows that the same bit circuit supports $\tau=1-\ab$.
This sharpening changes no gate, role, frame, or tape algorithm.
Using just the linear Taylor term is insufficient for this sharper
numerical value; the verifier specifically checks that distinction.

\section{Completed layers and two explicit parameter witnesses}
The retained compact-control recurrence gives the internal, leaf, and
reservation exponents
\[
 \chi=\tau+(1-\beta)\max\{\sigma-\tau,0\},\qquad
 \chi_{\rm leaf}=\sigma+\beta(1-\sigma),\qquad
 \chi_{\rm reserve}=\max\{1-c,0\}.
\]
Here $\ac>\ab$, so $\sigma<\tau$ and $\chi=\tau$.  Retaining the
old formula without the maximum would incorrectly overstate the gain.
We require
\[
 \max\{\tau,\sigma,\chi\}<\lambda<\lambda'<1,
 \qquad
 \max\{\chi_{\rm leaf},\chi_{\rm reserve}\}<\lambda'.
\]
For rational $0<\beta<1$ and $\zeta>0$ the guard has
$C_1=5-4\beta+\zeta$.  Its node constant is evaluated using the
\emph{new} $m,W,s$, not the baseline complex counts.  The finite circuit
bound in the construction and $2\le s<m^5$ supply its hypotheses.

We use $c=1$, so $K=d$.  The compact front and back fields each reserve
$O(\log p)$ coordinate chunks, and the row reservations use $O(\log d)$
chunks.  Their total remains $o(d)$.  Reservation preprocessing has
exponent zero, while $\epsilon(1+c)=2\epsilon<1$ ensures
$K=o(\ell)$ with $\ell=\Theta(p^{1-\epsilon})$.  Thus $c=1$ is
within the retained layout proof; no free temporary address coordinates
or new tape operation are being assumed.

\subsection{A readable witness}
Using $\ab=296/10^{11}$, choose
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \epsilon=\frac{19999}{100000},\quad c=1,\quad
 \beta=\frac14,\quad\zeta=\frac1{100},\quad
 C_1=\frac{401}{100},\quad\delta=\frac1{10^6},\\
 \lambda=1-\frac{5919}{2\cdot10^{12}},\qquad
 \lambda'=1-\frac{2959}{10^{12}},\qquad
 \kappa_0=\frac{59}{10^{11}}=5.9\cdot10^{-10}.
\end{gathered}
\]
The leaf saving is $(1-\beta)\ac=3\cdot10^{-9}$, strictly larger
than $1-\lambda'=2.959\cdot10^{-9}$.  The internal saving is
$\ab=2.96\cdot10^{-9}>1-\lambda>1-\lambda'$.  Also
$\epsilon C_1<1$.  The seven assembly margins are
\begin{align*}
 g_1&=1-\epsilon(1+c), &g_2&=\epsilon c\ab,\\
 g_3&=\epsilon(1-\lambda'), &g_4&=\ab(1-\epsilon),\\
 g_5&=\tfrac14-\delta-\tfrac54\epsilon,
 &g_6&=1-\delta-\epsilon,\qquad g_7=\epsilon.
\end{align*}
Exact arithmetic gives
\[
 \min_i g_i=g_3=\frac{59177041}{10^{17}}
              =5.9177041\cdot10^{-10}>\kappa_0.
\]
All remaining setup and precision constraints are checked in the companion
certificate.  This already proves the conditional corollary $\kappa=2^{-31}$.

\subsection{The strongest supplied witness}
Use the sharpened $\ab=29646101715649/10^{22}$ and set
$t=10^{-12}$.  Choose
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \epsilon=\frac{399999999}{2000000000},\quad c=1,\quad
 \beta=\frac1{10},\quad\zeta=\frac1{100},\quad
 C_1=\frac{461}{100},\quad\delta=10^{-12},\\
 \lambda=1-\ab(1-t/2),\qquad
 \lambda'=1-\ab(1-t),\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{5929220328}{10^{19}}.
\end{gathered}
\]
Now $(1-\beta)\ac=3.6\cdot10^{-9}>\ab$, so the leaves do not
limit $1-\lambda'$.  The minimum margin is
\[
 G=\epsilon\ab(1-t)
   =\frac{11858440656601639843694386501715649}{2\cdot10^{43}}.
\]
In particular,
\[
 G-\kappa
 =\frac{601639843694386501715649}{2\cdot10^{43}}>0,
 \qquad g_5=\frac{39}{62500000000}>G.
\]
The other five margins are also strictly larger than $\kappa$.
The guard exponent obeys
\[
 \epsilon C_1=\frac{184399999539}{200000000000}<1.
\]
The unchanged Gaussian choice $\alpha=\lceil(32db)^{1/4}\rceil$
has exponent $(1+\epsilon)/4$, and
$\gamma=2d\alpha^2$ has exponent $1/2+3\epsilon/2<1$.
Prime selection retains $1-2\epsilon>0$.  The record suffix still grows
faster than any fixed polynomial in $p$, and all rational exponent
comparisons are fixed finite machine computations.  The strict gap
$G-\kappa$ absorbs the remaining fixed powers of $\log p$.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional extension]
Under the retained upstream and compact-control interfaces identified above,
the explicitly constructed finite network and parameter witness yield
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-5929220328/10^{19}}\right).
\]
The saving lies strictly between $2^{-31}$ and $2^{-30}$ and exceeds the
baseline $83/10^{12}$ by the factor
$741152541/103750000\approx7.14364$.
\end{theorem}

\section{A scoped ceiling and the next obstruction}
This construction moves the bottleneck to the bit recurrence.  Write
$q=1-\lambda'$.  The strict layer constraints imply $q<\ab$.
For any feasible saving,
\[
 \kappa<\epsilon q,\qquad
 \kappa<\frac14-\delta-\frac54\epsilon.
\]
Eliminating $\epsilon$ yields
\[
 \kappa<\frac{q(1-4\delta)}{5+4q}
         <\frac{\ab}{5+4\ab}.
\]
Conversely, with this complex saving, $\beta=1/4$, $\zeta=1/100$ and
$c=1$, take $q\uparrow\ab$, $\delta\downarrow0$ and
$\epsilon=(1-4\delta)/(5+4q)$; choose $\lambda$ strictly between
$\tau$ and $\lambda'$.  The leaf and guard constraints stay slack and
the other assembly margins do not bind.  Thus $\ab/(5+4\ab)$ is the
parameter supremum for this fixed certified bit saving and these retained
constraints.  It is about $5.9292203291\cdot10^{-10}$ for the sharpened
$\ab$.  Replacing $\ab$ by the exact bit-graph saving and using rational
logarithm enclosures changes only the last reported digits.

This ceiling is not a lower bound on integer multiplication, nor an
optimality result for all finite networks.  To cross the next dyadic
milestone $2^{-30}$ within this approach, one needs a stronger bit
primitive or a stronger downstream estimate, such as a changed Gaussian
cost bound.  Further reducing the complex circuit alone does not remove
the present restriction.

\section{Reproduction and verification boundary}
The new implementation uses only Python's standard library:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 scripts/complex_pair_star.py
python3 scripts/complex_pair_star_parameters.py
python3 scripts/make_complex_pair_star_patch.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_complex_pair_star*.py' -v
git apply --check --directory=upstream patches/complex-pair-star-31.patch
\end{verbatim}
The main certificate records all $263$ batches, all $77{,}454$ additions,
all $469{,}294$ output uses, every one of the $4{,}096{,}576$ ordered
source--target coefficients, both compiled frame directions, residual unit
witnesses, exact recurrence inequalities, and all seven assembly margins.
The tests additionally exercise the complete signed invocation, its inverse,
and a three-shear signed exchange with reused arbitrary dyadic scratch at
$h=8$.  Real dyadic tests suffice for the complex scalar identities because
all operations are linear with real dyadic coefficients and hence act
independently on real and imaginary parts.

The new patch applies independently to the unmodified pinned manuscript.
It incorporates the previously retained compact-control modifications and
inserts this new complex interface and the sharpened bit comparison.
The verifier does not implement the complete multiplication machine, and
finite tests do not prove uniform Turing-tape estimates or the upstream
analytic identities.  Those remain the explicitly stated proof dependencies.
No new unrestricted optimality, independent human review, or practical
performance claim is made.
